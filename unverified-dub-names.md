# Unverified بباي الطيبين dub names — scratch file

These are pure guesses, not facts. No source states them outright — they were
inferred from indirect context. Do not use any of these as a question's
correct answer, and do not copy them into CLAUDE.md, until each one is
actually sourced (a real Arabic-language reference confirming the mapping).

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-08-01 so a future session doesn't mistake them
for verified project memory.

- **صنديد = Greymon** (أبطال الديجيتال / Digimon). Inferred only from the
  phrase "Amjad's companion evolved to صنديد at adult level" — no source
  states directly that صنديد is Greymon's Arabic dub name.
- **Digimon companions with no known owner**: أزرق، سلحوف، صقر. These three
  are confirmed to be part-two companion characters in أبطال الديجيتال, but
  which human character each one belongs to is not known or sourced.
  (For contrast: خرموش (هند) and صدى (وسيم) DO have a known owner and are
  still recorded in CLAUDE.md.)
